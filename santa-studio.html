Connect to load the clips stored on your Santa. Tap a clip to play it; star it to add it to the rotation the Santa cycles through.

ℹ︎
Bluetooth only lets the app send audio to the Santa — there's no command to pull its built-in clips back off, so I can't sample its onboard voice directly. What this studio does instead: take a clean recording (or a file), tidy and shape it, and send it over as a new clip. For a true Santa-style voice you can plug in your own ElevenLabs key below (the app uses that service under the hood).
